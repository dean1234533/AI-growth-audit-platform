## Decide what you need to learn

A small-business owner may need an understandable list of fixes. A developer may need resource-level performance diagnostics. An agency may need a crawl of thousands of pages, client reporting and keyword positions. Check the tool’s scope before treating two scores as comparable.

Growth Audit provides a free, no-account audit with SEO, performance, automated accessibility, trust, mobile, conversion and relevant local-business checks. Its report prioritises actions and can be downloaded as a PDF. It is a bounded audit, not a replacement for every specialist measurement.

## Website graders and quick health checks

Tools such as [HubSpot Website Grader](https://website.grader.com/) and [Seobility SEO Checker](https://www.seobility.net/en/seocheck/) provide quick diagnostic checks. UK-focused tools such as [GrowthCheck](https://growthcheck.co.uk/) and [SEO Health Check](https://seohealthcheck.co.uk/) also address small-business website review searches.

Compare which pages and categories are checked, whether evidence accompanies each recommendation and what requires registration or a paid plan. Avoid choosing by the number of checks alone: several minor checks can be less useful than one serious problem affecting a customer journey.

## Specialist crawlers and monitoring platforms

[Screaming Frog SEO Spider](https://www.screamingfrog.co.uk/seo-spider/) focuses on technical website crawling. [Sitechecker](https://sitechecker.pro/) combines website auditing with monitoring, keyword tracking and other SEO data tools.

A crawler can inspect a much larger URL inventory than a bounded quick audit. Ranking tools and Search Console answer questions about search visibility that a page health score does not. Confirm crawl limits, integrations, exports and current terms on the official pages; this guide does not assert competitor prices or equal feature coverage.

## Performance tools

[Google PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev/) offers performance diagnostics and, where available, real-user field data. [GTmetrix](https://gtmetrix.com/) provides performance analysis, with testing and monitoring options described on its current site.

Use a specialist speed tool when you need to investigate individual requests, loading behaviour or changes under specific test conditions. Growth Audit uses PageSpeed data when available as one part of a wider review. See the [PageSpeed comparison](/compare/google-pagespeed-vs-ai-website-audit) and [GTmetrix comparison](/compare/gtmetrix-vs-ai-website-audit).

## Questions to ask before relying on a report

- Does the report inspect the submitted page, a sample or the complete site?
- Does it distinguish measured, detected, estimated and unavailable evidence?
- Are the recommendations specific enough to act on and verify?
- Does a paid plan add monitoring, more sites, technical crawl capacity or reporting customisation?
- Is authenticated content inspected, and is that appropriate for the task?
- Can you export a report and repeat the same test after a fix?

## Combine a diagnosis with real outcome data

A website audit identifies issues to investigate; it does not prove why a particular keyword ranks or predict a fixed number of extra enquiries. Use your own Search Console, analytics and business records to establish a baseline and measure change.
