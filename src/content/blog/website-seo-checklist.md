SEO can feel overwhelming when every article seems to reference a different set of best practices. In reality, for most small business websites, ranking well comes down to getting a manageable list of fundamentals right. Here's the complete checklist.

## On-page fundamentals

- [ ] **Unique title tag** on every page, ideally 30-60 characters, including your primary keyword naturally
- [ ] **Unique meta description** on every page, 50-160 characters, written to earn the click (not just describe the page)
- [ ] **Single H1 heading** per page that clearly states the page's topic
- [ ] **Logical heading hierarchy** — H2s and H3s that don't skip levels
- [ ] **Descriptive alt text** on every meaningful image (skip purely decorative ones)
- [ ] **Canonical URL** set to avoid duplicate content issues

## Technical foundations

- [ ] **robots.txt** present and not accidentally blocking pages you want indexed
- [ ] **sitemap.xml** present and submitted to Google Search Console
- [ ] **HTTPS/SSL** enabled site-wide
- [ ] **Mobile-friendly** — responsive layout, readable text without zooming, adequately sized tap targets
- [ ] **Fast page load** — see our [website speed guide](/blog/how-to-improve-your-website-speed) for specifics
- [ ] **No broken internal links**

## Structured data (schema.org)

Structured data helps Google understand your content well enough to show rich results — star ratings, FAQ dropdowns, business details — directly in search results.

- [ ] **Organization or LocalBusiness schema** on your homepage
- [ ] **FAQPage schema** on pages with genuine FAQ content
- [ ] **BreadcrumbList schema** for clear site navigation
- [ ] **Article schema** on blog posts

## Content quality

- [ ] Every page has genuinely unique content — not a copy-pasted template with only the location or service name changed
- [ ] Content directly answers what someone searching that term actually wants to know
- [ ] Service pages are specific — a dedicated page per core service outperforms one page trying to cover everything

## Local SEO (if you serve a local area)

- [ ] **Google Business Profile** claimed, fully completed, and actively maintained
- [ ] **NAP consistency** — your name, address and phone number match exactly across your website, Google Business Profile and any directory listings
- [ ] **Dedicated location pages** if you genuinely serve multiple distinct towns or areas
- [ ] **Reviews** displayed on-site and actively collected

## Off-page and ongoing

- [ ] Genuine backlinks from relevant, reputable sites (quality over quantity)
- [ ] Regular content updates — a stale site with no activity in years is a weaker signal than one showing ongoing activity
- [ ] Monitoring via Google Search Console for crawl errors and search performance
